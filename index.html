TS-WAM

Temporal Latent Actions and World-Action Coupling for Vision-Language Robot Control

A teacher-student world-action model that learns temporal latent actions from robot demonstrations and action-free human videos, coupling future visual prediction with action generation.

Evaluated on LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, SimplerEnv, and real-world Franka Research 3 and RevoTron manipulation.

arXiv: 2610.21922 ↗Jun–Sep 2026
Method & findings +

The VLM-JEPA framework uses an exponential moving average (EMA) target branch to learn temporal latent actions. Delta-LWM predicts future visual features that guide action generation. Temporal representations, visual dynamics, and control are optimized jointly without separate latent-action or world-model pretraining.

Experiments show improved generalization and robustness across simulated benchmarks and real robots. The EMA target branch is removed at inference.

World models · Long-horizon planningAAAI 2027 · Under review

Drift-JEPA

Drift-Aware Joint-Embedding Predictive World Models for Long-Horizon Visual Planning

Addresses compounding error in autoregressive world models through context- and action-conditioned drift risk estimation, structured perturbations, and recovery training.

Improves latent prediction and goal-conditioned planning across six manipulation and navigation tasks in MetaWorld, Push-T, and Wall.

Jun–Aug 2026

Method & findings +

During training, action-conditioned latent world models receive clean encoder features; during planning, they repeatedly consume their own predictions. This mismatch accumulates drift over long horizons.

Drift-JEPA stores observed drift at different prediction horizons in a replay buffer. A drift risk estimator identifies vulnerable features, and structured perturbations train the predictor to recover from drifted inputs. Drift-specific components are removed after training, preserving the original model and planner at inference.
